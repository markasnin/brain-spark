# Brain Spark

Brain Spark is a Hebrew math practice website and progressive web app for Israeli students in grades א׳–ז׳. Select a grade to see its practice topics, worked examples, exams, and minigames.

## Curriculum

The original question templates in `curriculum.js` use three levels of difficulty. They supplement the grade-specific arithmetic and visual geometry generators. The examples include everyday situations such as giving change in shekels, planning transport, reading a survey, scaling a map, and calculating the cost of a group activity.

| Grade | Selected practice topics |
| --- | --- |
| א׳ | Counting and place value, addition and subtraction stories, length, simple surveys |
| ב׳ | Hundreds, equal groups, halves and quarters, money and time |
| ג׳ | Rounding and estimation, unit fractions, interpreting remainders, measurement conversions |
| ד׳ | Place value and rounding, equivalent fractions, order of operations, multi-step word problems |
| ה׳ | Common fractions, decimal operations, area, interpreting averages |
| ו׳ | Multiplying and dividing fractions and decimals, percentages, ratio and map scale, circle measurements, volume |
| ז׳ | Coordinate grids, signed numbers, angles, transformations, powers and roots, algebraic expressions, equations, relative frequency and probability, Pythagoras, areas and solids, graph reading |
